# Capstone — Design, predict, fly, defend
Develop a modest single-stage model rocket around an unchanged certified commercial motor. Set budget, field, altitude, recovery, mass, and measurement requirements first.

Deliver: requirements; alternatives and rationale; drawings/component list; mass budget; stability and recovery analysis; simulation with assumptions and sensitivity checks; independent predictions; design review; construction record; approved flight plan; raw observations; postflight report.

Proceed to flight only after unresolved review issues and the flight gate are closed. If conditions prevent flight, submit the completed design and simulation portfolio and keep flight validation explicitly pending.

Defend what was measured, what was inferred, and what remains unknown. Evaluate achievement against the original requirements, including cost and recoverability.
